NOTAKILLITE / LOCAL AUDIO

Your audio processor is ready.

Export lecture recordings from your live Notability web account. Speech cleanup and MP3 conversion happen on this computer.

  1. Open Chrome or Edge → Extensions → enable Developer mode.
  2. Choose Load unpacked and select this project's extension folder.
  3. Go to Notability and sign in normally. Your notes must be available in Notability Cloud.
  4. Reload the Notability tab once, then open the Notakillite extension. Choose a note and press Export audio.

Keep the processor window open while exporting. Files appear in the exports folder next to the launcher.

Cleanup reduces steady noise and evens out volume; it does not isolate one speaker. The app does not upload recordings to an AI service.
